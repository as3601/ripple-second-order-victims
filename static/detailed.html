How Ripple works

When a bridge collapses, everyone looks at the bridge. But that bridge was also an ambulance route, a school-bus route and a medicine supply line. Ripple finds the people hurt by those hidden dependencies: the second-order victims.

1st orderWhat physically brokee.g. bridge cut, road flooded
2nd orderWho depended on ite.g. ambulances 26 min late
3rd orderWho gets hurt nexte.g. hospital overflows, elderly miss medicines

Under the hood

  1. City graph: 27 junctions and 36 roads with real OpenStreetMap locations and OSRM drive times, plus hospitals, ambulances, medicine depots, schools, workplaces and 11 residential areas.
  2. Break and re-route: the event damages the graph, and every dependency (home → hospital, depot → pharmacy, home → school, home → work) is re-routed and compared with normal.
  3. Rank: each consequence is scored by severity × people × vulnerability × how soon it lands.
  4. Explain: a local AI writes the briefing and suggests hidden risks. Your ✓/✗ feedback re-weights future predictions.

Prototype: road layout, populations and bed counts are estimates.
